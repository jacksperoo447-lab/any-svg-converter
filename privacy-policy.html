Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 19, 2026

At AnySVG (accessible at anysvg.com), your privacy is our highest priority. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect, how it is used, and our commitment to protecting your digital rights.

1. Zero-Upload Architecture (100% Client-Side Processing)

Unlike traditional conversion services that upload your private photos, artwork, and confidential company logos to cloud servers, AnySVG operates 100% locally inside your web browser using client-side JavaScript, WebAssembly, and the HTML5 Canvas API.

  • Your files are never transferred over the internet to our servers.
  • We do not store, copy, inspect, or retain your images in any database or file system.
  • When you close or refresh your browser tab, all temporary memory used by your image is immediately purged.

2. Information We Collect

Because we do not require account registration or user logins, we collect only minimal, non-personally identifiable analytical data standard across web servers:

  • Log Files: IP address (anonymized), browser type, referring pages, date and time stamp, and general geographic region. This data is strictly used for site performance optimization and security analysis.
  • Contact Form Data: If you voluntarily reach out via our Contact page, we receive your email and message solely to respond to your inquiry.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

We use third-party advertising companies, including Google AdSense, to serve advertisements when you visit our website. These companies may use cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or through the Network Advertising Initiative.

4. Cookies & Web Beacons

AnySVG uses essential cookies solely to save your UI preferences (such as your chosen tracing mode). Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits. You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent.

5. GDPR and CCPA Compliance

In accordance with the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you retain the right to request access to your personal information, request erasure, or object to data processing. Because we do not store user files or personal identities, we maintain no data archives of your creative work.
