About AnySVG

Empowering designers, engineers, and digital crafters with private, client-side vector precision.

Our Mission

Founded in 2026, AnySVG was created to solve a persistent frustration experienced by millions of digital creators: most online SVG converters are neither true vectorizers nor respect user privacy.

Traditional conversion websites force users to upload proprietary artwork, family photographs, or confidential company logos to unverified cloud servers. In addition, many of these tools merely package pixelated bitmaps inside a dummy SVG tag rather than calculating genuine mathematical curves.

We engineered AnySVG around two non-negotiable principles:

  1. True Vector Mathematics: Real contour tracing, color clustering, and cubic Bezier curve fitting so your graphics scale indefinitely without fuzziness.
  2. Absolute Browser Privacy: Every single pixel is processed locally in your own device's browser memory using modern web standards. No server uploads. Ever.

Who Uses AnySVG?

🎨 UI/UX & Graphic Designers

Converting raster PNG icons, badges, and illustrations into layered SVG assets for Figma, Sketch, and Adobe Illustrator.

✂️ Crafters & Cricut Makers

Generating smooth, single-path cut lines for vinyl plotters, laser engravers, and embroidery digitizing software.

💻 Web Developers

Eliminating heavy raster image assets and replacing them with ultra-light, responsive SVG code for instant site loading.

Open & Transparent Engineering

AnySVG leverages open algorithmic foundations including the Douglas-Peucker path simplification theorem and adaptive color quantization. Our platform is completely free to use, supported by clean, respectful advertising through Google AdSense.
