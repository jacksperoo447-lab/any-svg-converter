About Converting WebP to Scalable Vector Graphics

WebP is Google's modern image format designed for efficient web delivery. While it compresses images much better than PNG and JPG, it remains a raster format bound to fixed pixel grids.

When you need to edit a WebP image inside Figma, Illustrator, or extract its geometry for a laser cutter or vinyl plotter, converting WebP to SVG gives you discrete vector nodes and curves that you can scale to any dimension without quality loss.
