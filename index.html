Why Choose Real Path Tracing Over Fake SVG Wrappers?

Many free tools on the web claim to convert PNG or JPG to SVG, but in reality, they merely encode your raster image as a Base64 string and embed it inside a <image> tag. When you zoom in, the image still blurs and pixelates, and it cannot be used for vinyl cutting, embroidery, or CNC machines.

AnySVG is different: Our built-in vectorization algorithm traces actual pixel contours, performs intelligent color quantization, and outputs genuine SVG <path> elements with clean curves. Whether you need vectors for logos, icons, t-shirt printing, or Cricut cutting machines, AnySVG delivers real vector output.
